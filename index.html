About Me

What do i do ?

I design systems that improve how organizations operate. I identify operational problems, understand stakeholder needs, and design practical systems that improve efficiency through technology, process redesign, and data-informed decision making.

Understanding systems before building solutions

My career began in Health Education, and I discovered a stronger interest in how systems influence outcomes. That curiosity led me to technology—not to code for its own sake, but to use software as a tool to solve organizational problems.

I focus on asking the right questions: Why is a process designed this way? Who experiences the most friction? Where does information slow down? Answers to these questions shape solutions people actually use.



Philosophy

People, process, and technology

I don't believe technology creates transformation alone. Transformation happens when people, processes, and technology align. My work prioritizes understanding the problem before designing the solution so teams adopt and benefit from the systems we build.

Approach

Discover • Analyze • Design • Implement

Discover

Speak with stakeholders, observe workflows, and question assumptions to understand the real problem.

Analyze

Map processes, identify bottlenecks, and prioritize user needs to define clear objectives.

Design

Translate requirements into practical systems that simplify workflows and improve visibility.

Implement

Build, iterate, validate with users, and improve continuously.
